# Forma Studio Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Applies to the native Forma Studio: Photo & Type app for iPhone and iPad, published by Anthony Peytchev.

## Your creations stay under your control

Photos, text, drawings, imported fonts, and projects are processed locally. The app does not upload your photos or creative briefs to the developer or a third-party AI service. AI writing uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models when available. There is no app account, advertising, tracking SDK, or developer-operated analytics service.

The app saves one current project in its local app storage. It stores the welcome-screen completion preference on your device. You can save additional projects through Apple's Files interface and export images to Photos or destinations you choose through the share sheet. Those destinations, iCloud/device backups, and services you choose have their own privacy policies. Deleting the app removes its local app storage; copies you exported or backed up may remain in those locations.

## Photo access

Apple's photo picker lets you choose which photos to import. The app requests permission to add images to your photo library only when you choose to save an export to Photos. You can manage that permission in Settings. You can still use the share sheet when Photos permission is unavailable.

## Purchases

Apple processes optional Forma Pro purchases through StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified transactions on your device to determine whether Pro is unlocked. The developer does not receive or store your payment card information and does not operate a server to collect purchase histories. Apple may process transaction, diagnostics, and App Store usage information under its own policies and your device settings.

## Support

Support is available through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/EmbeddedTony/forma-studio-support/issues). Support requests are voluntary and public. Do not include private photos, project files, passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal information. GitHub processes information you submit under its own privacy policy. You can edit or remove your own issue content using GitHub's controls.

## Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, [open a support issue](https://github.com/EmbeddedTony/forma-studio-support/issues/new) without including sensitive personal information.
